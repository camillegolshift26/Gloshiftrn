The problem

Compliance in aesthetics lives in too many places.

A new injector starts Monday. Her license is in an email thread, her BLS card is a photo on someone's phone, and the medical director's sign-off is a PDF nobody can find. Multiply that by every provider, every renewal, every state requirement.

Generic HR software wasn't built for medical spas, and spreadsheets don't send reminders. The gaps only show up when an inspector, an insurer, or a lapsed certification finds them first.

Capabilities

Everything a new hire needs, in one record.

Onboarding checklists

Role-specific steps for injectors, aestheticians, and front desk, so nothing is skipped on day one.

License & certification tracking

RN, NP, PA, BLS, and device trainings stored with their expiration dates and flagged before they lapse.

Medical director sign-offs

Collect and store standing orders, delegations, and competency sign-offs alongside each provider.

Workplace compliance

Required policies, acknowledgments, and trainings, with a clear view of who has completed what.

State requirements

Keep state-mandated notices and pamphlets on file and acknowledged for every employee.

Attention at a glance

One view of what's incomplete, expiring, or overdue across your whole team.

How it works

From offer letter to fully compliant.

  1. Add your team member

    Enter their role and start date. GloShift builds the checklist that role requires.

  2. Collect the documents

    Licenses, certifications, trainings, and sign-offs are uploaded once and stored in their record.

  3. Stay ahead of renewals

    GloShift tracks every expiration date and tells you what needs attention before it becomes a problem.

Who it's for

Built for aesthetic practices.

For you if

  • You own or manage a med spa, aesthetic clinic, or injectables practice
  • You employ RNs, NPs, PAs, or aestheticians with licenses and certifications to keep current

Not for you if

Our story

Built by a nurse who's seen the gaps.

I'm Camille Colapinto, a BSN-prepared RN with nine years in aesthetic medicine. For four of those years I was a national clinical educator, training practices across the country on injectables and devices.

Walking into clinic after clinic, I saw the same thing: talented teams, and compliance held together by memory and good intentions. Expired certifications, missing sign-offs, onboarding that depended on whoever happened to be at the front desk.
